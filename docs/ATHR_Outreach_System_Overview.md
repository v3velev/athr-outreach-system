# athr - Outreach System Overview

## What this gives you

A predictable, always-on flow of conversations with Bulgarian companies that need
to hire - so your pipeline fills itself instead of depending on referrals or luck.

It runs as **two outreach engines** side by side: one catches companies **the
moment they start hiring**, the other reaches **the entire market**.

## Why two systems

Companies hire on two timelines. A few are hiring **right now** and want help
today. Most aren't hiring this week, but **will be soon** - and you want to be the
name they already know when that moment comes. Each engine covers one timeline.

| | **System 1 - Active Hiring** | **System 2 - Full Market** |
|---|---|---|
| Targets | Companies posting a job today | Every relevant company in Bulgaria |
| Signal | High intent (hiring now) | Coverage (the whole market) |
| Timing | Same-day, perfectly timed | Always-on awareness |
| Runs | Automatically, every day | In waves, refreshed every few months |

---

## System 1 - Active Hiring Outreach
### The daily engine

**What it is:** an automated system that watches the main Bulgarian job boards
every day and reaches out to a company the moment it posts a role you can fill.

**How it works** - on its own, every day:

1. **Scans** new job postings across the country.
2. **Filters** to the right ones - your kind of company, real employers (not
   other recruitment agencies), Bulgaria-based, skipping the saturated sectors.
3. **Finds** the right person and a **verified** email.
4. **Writes** a short, personalised Bulgarian email that references their exact
   open position.
5. **Sends** it - one company, one message, never the same company twice.

**Why it works:** timing. You reach them on the day they realise they need
someone - when your offer is most relevant and replies are highest.

---

## System 2 - Full-Market Outreach
### The coverage engine

**What it is:** a broad campaign to every relevant company in Bulgaria - not just
the ones hiring today, but the whole market. The list is already built.

**How it works:**

1. **A short email sequence** introduces athr and your result-based offer - you
   only pay when a hire is made - and goes out over a couple of months.
2. **When the campaign finishes, you choose:**
   - **Re-send** to the same list to stay top of mind, or
   - **Refresh** the list to pull in new companies.
3. **Every 4-6 months, refresh** so you're always reaching new businesses.

**Why it works:** coverage plus repetition. Most companies aren't hiring the exact
day you email them - but when they are, **athr is the name they remember**.

---

## What to expect

- **System 1** gives you a **steady daily stream** of warm, well-timed leads - the
  companies hiring that day that fit your profile.
- **System 2** gives you **reach** - your name in front of the whole market, with
  interest building each time the sequence lands.
- Everything goes out at a **controlled pace to verified contacts only** - quality
  over blast, so your sending stays healthy and lands in the inbox.

---

## What's handled for you

- **Verified emails only** - every address is checked before anything is sent.
- **No company contacted twice** - the system remembers everyone reached.
- **No competitors, wrong country, or junk** - filtered out automatically.
- **Your voice** - every message sounds like athr: human, Bulgarian, built around
  the result-based offer.
- **Replies come straight to your inbox** - ready for a call.

---

## How the two work together

System 1 brings **warm, perfectly-timed** conversations every day. System 2 keeps
athr **in front of the whole market** so the pipeline never runs dry. Short-term
wins and long-term coverage, at the same time.

## What you actually do

Very little. The daily engine **runs itself**. For the full-market campaign, you
simply decide when to re-send or refresh the list every few months. Interested
replies come to you - you take the calls and close the deals.
